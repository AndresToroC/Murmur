Launcher mask
The name

Murmur = murmullo (Spanish for murmur)

A low, continuous, barely audible sound. The word repeats itself (mur·mur), like a breath or a snore that comes back. It works the same in Spanish and English and brings a double M that calls for symmetry and rhythm.

The product

An instrument that listens at night

  • It records only what stands out from the silence: snoring, talking, coughing.
  • Everything local and private: the logo must not look like a spy microphone.
  • Night blue, thin borders and monospaced figures: more meter than wellness app.

Technical limits

What the logo has to withstand

  • 108 dp adaptive icon: everything inside the 66 dp circle, because every launcher crops differently.
  • monochrome layer (Android 13+): a single ink; today it reuses the color icon.
  • A white 24 dp glyph for the notification and the status bar, and another for the Quick Settings tile.
  • Avoid the worn-out: microphone, moon with stars, "Zzz", a lone equalizer.

All seven together, at real size

Icons at 48 dp on a dark and a light background, and the 24 dp glyphs in a status bar.

My recommendation

Ranked by how well they meet three things at once: recognizable at 48 dp, survives in a single ink, and could only belong to Murmur.

Either one can be refined in the next round: stroke widths tuned per size, the 108 dp VectorDrawable with its own monochrome layer, the notification and Quick Settings glyphs, and the logo with the "Murmur" title in Inter Tight.
